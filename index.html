About

I’m a UI/UX Designer and Frontend Developer with over 9 years of experience building and optimizing websites that balance clean design, usability, and performance. I specialize in creating conversion-focused landing pages and scalable websites using tools like WordPress and Webflow.

My work goes beyond visuals—I focus on how users interact, how content performs, and how design decisions impact business results. From design to deployment, I’ve led and supported projects that require both creative thinking and solid technical execution.

I also explore content and marketing through short-form video and AI-assisted workflows, helping streamline processes and improve output. I’m always looking for better ways to build, optimize, and deliver meaningful digital experiences.
